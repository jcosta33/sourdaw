# EQ — Equalizer

EQ is a three-band equalizer. Add it to a track, then open it in the inspector.

**Type** Audio effect · **Category** EQ & Filter · **Load from** Sidebar → Effects → **EQ & Filter**
→ **EQ**

The inspector title is the device name **EQ**. **Back to track** returns to the strip. The switch
next to the title bypasses EQ.

Each EQ on a track keeps its own knobs. A second EQ is not a second view of the first.

## At a glance

- Three bands: **Low Band**, **Mid Band**, **High Band**. Each has **Gain**, **Freq**, and **Q**.
- **Frequency Response** draws the curve. Drag a band left and right for frequency, up and down for
  gain.

## First moves

1. Select a track.
2. Open Sidebar → Effects → **EQ & Filter** and click **EQ**.
3. Open that **EQ** in the inspector.
4. Drag **Mid Gain** up on a vocal, or drag the middle of the curve up.

## Frequency Response

Heading **Frequency Response**. The picture is the three bands summed. Drag on a band to set that
band's **Freq** and **Gain**. **Q** is only the knobs below.

## Low Band

| Control      | Range        | Default | What it does               |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------- | -------------------------- |
| **Low Gain** | −24 to 24 dB | 0 dB    | Boost or cut.              |
| **Low Freq** | 20 to 500 Hz | 100 Hz  | Centre of the band.        |
| **Low Q**    | 0.1 to 10    | 1       | Width. Higher is narrower. |

## Mid Band

| Control      | Range           | Default | What it does               |
| ------------ | --------------- | ------- | -------------------------- |
| **Mid Gain** | −24 to 24 dB    | 0 dB    | Boost or cut.              |
| **Mid Freq** | 200 Hz to 8 kHz | 1 kHz   | Centre of the band.        |
| **Mid Q**    | 0.1 to 10       | 1       | Width. Higher is narrower. |

## High Band

| Control       | Range           | Default | What it does               |
| ------------- | --------------- | ------- | -------------------------- |
| **High Gain** | −24 to 24 dB    | 0 dB    | Boost or cut.              |
| **High Freq** | 2 kHz to 20 kHz | 8 kHz   | Centre of the band.        |
| **High Q**    | 0.1 to 10       | 1       | Width. Higher is narrower. |

## Presets

There is no EQ preset browser. The nine knobs save with the project, per EQ.

## Automation and control

All nine knobs appear as automation lanes.

> [!WARNING]
> **Not yet active.** A **Low Q** or **High Q** lane does not move that band on bounce. Turning the
> knobs still changes playback. **Mid Q** lanes do follow on bounce.
