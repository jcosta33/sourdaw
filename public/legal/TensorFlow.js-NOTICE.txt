- @tensorflow/tfjs-converter 4.22.0 — Apache-2.0

The complete Apache-2.0 text is in Apache-2.0.txt. The exact seedrandom and
bundled Alea MIT notices are in seedrandom-MIT.txt.

The tfjs-core package declares node-fetch for Node.js IO, but its browser map
replaces that module with false. Sourdaw's worker supplies an in-memory IO
handler and performs no network fetch. Type-only packages are not runtime code.

This notice applies to runtime code only. The separately downloaded Magenta
DDSP checkpoint artifacts have their own permission and provenance record and
are not described here as Apache-2.0 or MIT.
