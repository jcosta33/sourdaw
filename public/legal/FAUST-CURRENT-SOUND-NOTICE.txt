Sourdaw stock Faust device definitions

Adapted from https://github.com/grame-cncm/faustlibraries/tree/36661b5efd702e0e21b3ad49864e10201aecc602
Files: oscillators.lib, filters.lib, analyzers.lib, reverbs.lib.
Corresponding local source and exact upstream hashes: src/helpers/faustCurrentSound.lib and faustCurrentSound.sources.json.

The current Faust compiler remains unmodified. The selected stock oscillator, filter and reverb laws retain prior device sound at saved control values.
Functions carry their upstream author/license declarations. GRAME sections and mono_freeverb retain the LGPL2.1-or-later Faust exception; Julius O. Smith sections retain STK4.3.
